Who We Are?

We are a company specialized in creating visual identities. This includes logo design, typography, color palettes, letterhead, slide/presentation templates, uniforms, brand manuals, and more. All of our projects are carefully planned to bring the best results for our clients. The visual identity of your brand defines your target audience and the value you can charge for your product or service. Your brand cannot be entrusted to just anyone. If you’re looking for quality service, I would be happy to work with you.

Company History

Hi! I'm Jack Kennedy, the founder of AITTO, a firm specializing in branding. I am a self-taught professional with over 8 years of experience in the field. I began my career as a web developer, spending a year and a half in the industry-it was during this time that I discovered branding, leading me to pivot completely to the area in 2019. Since then, I have continuously refined my skills through both professional experience and specialized courses in Front-End and Back-End development, photography, sound design, design (with a focus on branding), and behavioral psychology.
